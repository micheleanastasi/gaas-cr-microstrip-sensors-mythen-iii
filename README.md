# Characterisation of GaAs:Cr Microstrip Sensors for Single Photon Counting

**Master's Thesis in Nuclear Engineering**  
**Michele Anastasi**  
Politecnico di Milano · Academic Year 2025/2026

Research conducted at the **Paul Scherrer Institute (PSI)**, Switzerland.

## Overview

This thesis investigates the performance of chromium-compensated gallium arsenide (GaAs:Cr) microstrip sensors coupled to the **MYTHEN III** single-photon-counting X-ray detector. The study focuses on the suitability of GaAs:Cr for X-ray detection at energies where conventional silicon sensors become less efficient.

Sensors with **50 µm and 100 µm strip pitches** are characterised, with particular attention to the interaction between sensor properties and readout electronics.

## Topics covered

- **Detector calibration and optimisation:** threshold scans, gain, equivalent noise charge (ENC), analogue front-end settings, temperature dependence and threshold trimming.
- **High-rate performance:** pulse pile-up, count-rate behaviour and readout trade-offs.
- **Charge transport and signal formation:** charge sharing, laser-based measurements and the crater effect.
- **Spatial resolution:** point spread function (PSF), modulation transfer function (MTF) and on-chip interpolation.
